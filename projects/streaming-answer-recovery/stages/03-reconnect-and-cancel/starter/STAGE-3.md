TypeScript `connect(url,state,onState,signal?):Promise<Answer>` bounds each connection to one MiB and marks failures interrupted. Go `Encode(StreamEvent)([]byte,error)`, `After(events,last)([]StreamEvent,error)`, `Handler(events)http.Handler`. No automatic reconnect loop is hidden; the caller chooses each connection.

Resume after the last accepted event, not the last partial byte.
