How often the real model's calls produce each shape (00-survey, 1 call per prompt, n=10 prompts per intent per model):
- one-action requests: 4b 9/10 one call (1/10 no call: "Write to a@x."); 8b 10/10 one call.
- two-action requests: 4b 8/10 two calls, 1/10 one call, 1/10 three calls; 8b 8/10 two, 1/10 three, 1/10 none. All calls had ids.
  So a batched review with 2+ actions (where one decision for two calls raises, B9, and a plain resume value raises, P8) is the normal case
  for a two-action request: 9/10 (4b) and 9/10 (8b) replies had 2 or more calls.
- Every tool call of the real model hit an interrupt-configured tool, so the jumping-middleware skip (J1/J3) fires on every such reply:
  in the case runs 6 of 6 J1/J3 runs ran send_email at step 0 before any decision.
- The edit that switches tools (B3) needs a reviewer's edit; the model cannot trigger it. Forced via the decision: 3/3.
