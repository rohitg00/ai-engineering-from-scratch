## stream_mode is rejected under v3

stream_events(version='v3', stream_mode='updates'):

raised builtins.TypeError: stream_events(version='v3') / astream_events(version='v3') do not accept stream_mode; v3 owns these (stream_mode is built from the transformer mux, subgraphs is forced True so nested namespaces flow through scoped muxes).

raised builtins.NotImplementedError: stream_events(version='v2') is not supported. Use astream_events() for v1/v2, or stream_events(version='v3') on a supported subclass.
