# Incident index

> The failures this manual tells as stories, with their symptom, their cause, their fix or status, and the section that explains each one.

The sections use real incidents as evidence. Each one comes from an SGLang or Miles issue, a pull request, a field report, or our own capture. This index lists them by part, so you can search it with a symptom you see in your own logs. A status of open or closed was checked on 2026-10-16.
