AI Engineering from Scratch

Privacy

AI Engineering from Scratch is a public, open-source curriculum. The lesson content is available without an account, payment, or learner profile. The site does not ask for passwords, API keys, or private project files.

What the site receives

Hosting and analytics providers may receive ordinary request data such as an IP address, browser user agent, referrer, requested URL, and approximate timing. This information is used for delivery, security, aggregate traffic measurement, and diagnosing broken pages. The project does not sell learner data or use lesson activity to build advertising profiles.

Local progress

Optional reading progress, theme preferences, and quiz state are stored in the browser when the site offers those controls. They are not required to read the curriculum and are not submitted as an account record by the static site. Clearing site storage removes that local state.

Third-party links

Lessons link to specifications, papers, repositories, and other resources. Those destinations have their own policies. Review them before submitting information. Questions about this policy can be raised through the contact page or the public repository.
