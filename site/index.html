Your agent is the tutor

You don't navigate this course. Your agent teaches it.

Most courses make you find your own way through them. This one ships its own tutor. Install the course skills into the coding agent you already use, and that agent places you, writes your study plan, and teaches each lesson in your terminal. You do not need to understand the curriculum to start.

  1. 01 npx skills add rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch

    Install the skills

    No clone needed. Works in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and any agent that reads SKILL.md.

  2. 02 /find-your-level

    Find your level

    Ten questions. Your agent maps what you already know to a starting phase, marks what you can skip, and estimates the hours.

  3. 03 /start-learning

    Get your plan

    Your agent saves your goal and placement to LEARNING.md, a study plan that lives next to your code.

  4. 04 /learn

    Learn every session

    A warm-up on the last lesson, the next lesson taught section by section in your terminal, a quiz, and your progress recorded.

Stuck on a topic or a bug? /course-guide points you to the exact lesson. Commands are shown for Claude Code. In Codex, type the skill name. In other agents, ask to use the skill by name. See every skill →

How every lesson works

Most AI material teaches in scattered pieces. A paper here, a fine-tuning post there, a flashy agent demo somewhere else. The pieces rarely line up. You ship a chatbot but can't explain its loss curve. You hook a function to an agent but can't say what attention does inside the model that's calling it.

This curriculum is the spine. Every phase, every published lesson, four languages: Python, TypeScript, Rust, Julia. Linear algebra at one end, autonomous swarms at the other. Every algorithm gets built from raw math first. Backprop. Tokenizer. Attention. Agent loop. By the time PyTorch shows up, you already know what it's doing under the hood.
