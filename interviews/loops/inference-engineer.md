A loop for a role that serves open models. Each round names one card. The interview-prep skill runs the rounds in this order.

- **screen**, 30 minutes: questions on the KV cache, batching and the metrics of a serving engine, from [inference and serving systems](../questions/inference-serving/README.md).
- **ml-depth**, 45 minutes: memory, quantization and the cost of long context, from the same card and its follow-ups.
- **debugging**, 45 minutes: one measured incident, read one listing at a time, from [incidents and scenarios](../scenarios/README.md).
- **system-design**, 60 minutes: design a batched inference API for a stated load and latency target.
- **coding**, 45 minutes: one practice task that builds a part of the serving path.
- **behavioral**, 15 minutes: a time you changed a decision because a measurement did not agree with you.
